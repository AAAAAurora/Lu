个人简介 Introduction

我目前为深圳大学人工智能学院2023级直博生，师从李坚强教授。聚焦具身智能中的机器人环境感知，围绕同步定位与建图开展研究，探索三维场景重建与语义理解的协同方法，提升机器人在复杂环境中的定位精度、建图质量与场景理解能力，为自主导航和智能交互提供支撑。以第一作者身份在NeurIPS、ICRA及Pattern Recognition等国际顶级会议与期刊上发表论文3篇，另有两项发明专利申请处于实质审查阶段。 I am a direct-entry Ph.D. student (2023 cohort) at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Shenzhen University, advised by Professor Jianqiang Li. My research focuses on robotic environmental perception in embodied intelligence, with an emphasis on simultaneous localization and mapping. I explore methods that jointly advance 3D scene reconstruction and semantic understanding to improve localization accuracy, mapping quality, and scene understanding in complex environments, supporting autonomous navigation and intelligent interaction. I have published three first-author papers in leading international conferences and journals, including NeurIPS, ICRA, and Pattern Recognition. Two invention patent applications are currently under substantive examination.
